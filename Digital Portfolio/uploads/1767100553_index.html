Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy quick dish perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combries beaten eggs cooked to prefecttion, optionally filled with your choice of cheese,vegetables, or meats.

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large
  • salt, to taste
  • Pepper to taste
  • 1tablespoon of butter
  • Optional fillings cheese,dicad vegetables, cooked meats,herbs
